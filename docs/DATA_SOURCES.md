**2022 edition expected counts:** 37 provinces, 416 kabupaten, 98 kota, 7,277 kecamatan, 8,498 kelurahan, 75,265 desa. Later Papua Barat Daya formation is not part of this historical edition. Do not mix current 38-province boundaries into 2022 code joins. A new 2025 Kemendagri decision superseded the 2022 version.
